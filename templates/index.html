How it Works

  • MediaPipe Hands: Tracks 21 hand landmarks in 3D space at 30 FPS.
  • Vector Posture Matching: Checks geometry between finger tips (e.g. 8, 12) and knuckle joints (6, 10) to classify fingers as open/closed.
  • OS Controls: Simulates hardware keys (`pyautogui`) and interacts directly with Windows audio APIs (`pycaw`) inside concurrent daemon threads.
